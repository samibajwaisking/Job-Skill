## Freelance / Consulting

**Headline Patterns That Work:**
- "Freelance [Skill] Expert | Helping [target audience] achieve [result]"
- "[Title] | [Top Skill] | Available for Projects"
- "AI Consultant & Educator | 2K+ Students | [Niche]"

**Signals Recruiters / Clients Look For:**
- "Open to Work" or "Open to Projects"
- Response time mentioned ("Responds within 24 hours")
- Specific results ("Helped 50+ clients", "Generated $X revenue")
- Portfolio link in Featured section

---

## Headline Formula (Highest Impact)

**Best Formula:**
`[Current Role] | [Top Skill #1] + [Top Skill #2] | [Value Proposition or Audience]`

**Examples:**
- "AI Educator & Web Developer | Claude API + No-Code | Teaching 2K+ Students in Pakistan"
- "Full Stack Developer | React + Node.js | Building SaaS Products for Startups"
- "Digital Marketing Consultant | SEO + Paid Ads | 10x ROI for E-commerce Brands"

## About Section Formula

Structure:
1. **Hook** (1-2 lines) — who you are + biggest achievement
2. **What you do** (2-3 lines) — specific services/skills
3. **Who you help** (1-2 lines) — target audience
4. **Proof** (2-3 lines) — numbers, results, testimonials
5. **CTA** (1 line) — "Let's connect" or "Message me"

---

## Skills Section Best Practices

- List **minimum 15 skills**, maximum 50
- Order: most important/in-demand first (they appear first to recruiters)
- Get **at least 5 endorsements** on top 3 skills
- Update every 6 months — remove outdated, add trending skills
- Include both **hard skills** (tools, languages) and **soft skills** (leadership, communication)
